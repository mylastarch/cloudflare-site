Missing since February 23, 1991

Where is
Vikki?

More than three decades after a young mother vanished from her Glenwood home, one question still waits for an answer.

On a winter night in 1991, Vikki L. Vukelich was at home in the 500 block of Tulip Drive. By morning, she was gone.

Vikki was a mother of two and the owner of a beauty salon in nearby South Holland. She had blonde hair, blue eyes, and was between 5 feet 1 inch and 5 feet 4 inches tall. She wore glasses or clear contact lenses—but both were reportedly left behind.

“She always kept in contact with her children.”

— Vikki’s older daughter, as reported in case records

The night she disappeared

According to Vikki’s husband, the couple argued about attending a party on February 23. He said he went to bed while Vikki slept on the couch. The next morning, an empty wine glass remained in the living room and Vikki was no longer there.

Her purse, driver’s license, clothing, shoes and vision aids were left at home. Her two daughters—then four and twelve—were there, too. A friend reported Vikki missing five days later.

Important

Some details in long-running missing-person cases come from witness statements and media reports. They should not be read as findings of guilt. No one has been charged in connection with Vikki’s disappearance.

A case that remains open

Authorities searched the former family home in 2003. Public case summaries say Vikki’s husband has been considered a prime suspect; he has not been charged. The case remains unsolved.

Time can make a case quieter, but it does not make a detail less important. A remembered conversation, an old photograph, a place once noticed—information that seemed insignificant in 1991 may matter today.
