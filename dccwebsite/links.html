    AUTISM

    April is Autism Acceptance Month

    CURRENT INFORMATION

    • Autism spectrum disorder is a developmental disability caused by differences in the brain.
    • About 1 in 31 eight-year-old children in 16 monitored U.S. communities were identified with autism in 2022.
    • Autism was identified more than three times as often among boys as among girls in those communities.
    • Autism occurs across all racial, ethnic, and socioeconomic groups.

    Source: CDC Autism Data and Statistics
